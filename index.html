Recording and loops work today in the lab. Friends and feeds come next.

How it works

  1. Hold to record.
  2. Let go to pause. Hold again.
  3. Six seconds, then it loops.

The lexicon

Hold-to-Loop
Hold to record, let go to pause.
Featherweight
A whole loop in about 100 KB.
Lifeboats
Two-second pieces. Lose one, the rest play.
Lighten Up
A busy relay drops frames, not seconds.
Real Seal
Signed on your phone, so edits show.
True Grain
Your camera’s grain, measured and put back.
Glow-Up
Tiny files, upscaled sharp as they play.
Honest Zoom
Sharper, never contradicting what was sent.
Smart Pixels
Pixels that fly home as a loop arrives.
Cosmic Courtesy
Important parts first, damage kept small.
